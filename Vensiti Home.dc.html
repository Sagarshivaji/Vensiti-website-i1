Questions

Before You Ask.

Is Vensiti a staffing agency or a consultancy?+

Both, deliberately. The same engineers who deliver DevOps and Quality Engineering work are the ones who technically screen every candidate we place — so the bar doesn't shift depending on which side of the business you're on.

Do you only work with large enterprises?+

No — most of our engagements are with Series B–D companies whose engineering demands have outgrown their headcount, alongside a handful of larger, established teams.

Which engagement model is right for us?+

Most people aren't sure on the first call, and that's fine — we help you land on the right one. Project-based, time & materials, dedicated team, or staff augmentation: see the full breakdown on How We Work.

How fast can we actually start?+

For hiring, you'll see qualified candidates within 48 hours of the intake call. For services, most engagements open with a two-week paid assessment before any longer commitment.

Do you guarantee your placements?+

Yes — a 90-day replacement window at no additional fee. Across our placements that rarely gets used: retention sits at 97% at twelve months.
