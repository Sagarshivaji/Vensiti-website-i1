What we actually do

Test automation & CI integration

Run a Playwright suite in parallel inside your pipeline, not bolted on after, so a red build blocks the merge, not the release.

Outcome: Faster feedback. Fewer flaky tests. Releases you can trust.

Performance & load testing

Build k6 load profiles from your real traffic patterns and run them before launch, not discovered during it.

Outcome: Fewer outages. Known limits. Confident launches.

Security & penetration testing

Run manual and automated checks against the OWASP Top 10, with findings ranked by exploitability, not just severity score.

Outcome: Real risks fixed first. Fewer surprises in audit.

Accessibility auditing

Run WCAG 2.2 AA audits with screen-reader testing, not just an automated scanner report nobody reads.

Outcome: Fewer legal risks. A product more people can use.

Questions people ask before signing

How much access do you need?+

Least-privilege, read-only to start. Broader access is added only where the assessment actually requires it — never broad admin rights by default.

Will you make changes to production?+

Not during the two-week assessment. You get read-only access and interviews only — any change afterward is proposed, reviewed, and approved by your team first.

How do you handle secrets and credentials?+

Through your existing vault or secrets manager. Test credentials are scoped to non-production environments wherever possible, and access is revoked the moment the engagement ends.

Do you test inside our existing CI/CD pipeline?+

Yes. Tests run inside your existing pipeline so a failure blocks a merge the same way it always has — no separate dashboard to remember to check.

Who owns the documentation?+

You do, from day one. Test plans, suites and decision records live in your own repo — nothing is held back at handover.

What do we receive after two weeks?+

A prioritized findings report, a risk assessment, test strategy recommendations, an implementation roadmap, documentation of what we found, and an estimate of the effort to fix it.

Can we stop after the assessment?+

Yes. The findings are yours either way — there's no obligation to continue into implementation.

What happens if we want Vensiti to implement the recommendations?+

We scope it as a follow-on engagement — project delivery, a managed pod, or staff augmentation, whichever fits how you want to work. See How We Work for the options.
