Questions people ask before signing

Do you replace our QA team or work alongside them?+

Alongside, by default. We build the suite and the practice with your QA team so they own it once we're gone — replacement is the exception, not the plan.

What test framework do you standardize on?+

Playwright for functional and end-to-end coverage, k6 for load — both open source, so nothing you're left maintaining requires a license we hold.

Do you test in our CI or a separate environment?+

Yours. Tests run inside your existing pipeline so a failure blocks a merge the same way it always has — no separate dashboard to remember to check.

Can we stop after the assessment?+

Yes. The findings are yours either way — there's no obligation to continue into implementation.
