Human-centered, as applied here
Every page opens by naming the person it is for, in their words, and offers one obvious next step for each of the three audiences. No page asks for information before it gives any. Copy is plain: no "synergy", no "solutions", no invented client logos. Photography, when it arrives, is candid and real — until then the About and team slots stay text-only rather than fake.
