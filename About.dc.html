Two practices. One standard.

Vensiti delivers technology services and places technology talent. Different work, same bar: would we hire this person, or ship this code, ourselves?

Why we exist

Most vendors pick one side of the problem.

Consultancies build things but don't hire for you. Recruiters hire but don't understand the systems your team is running. We started Vensiti because the same shortage — engineers who can be trusted with production — shows up on both sides of that line.

Every engineer who screens a candidate for Hire Talent also ships work on a client engagement. That's not a slogan — it's why a "Senior DevOps Engineer" posting and a DevOps engagement get judged by the same person.

How we operate

Four things we don't compromise on

Real numbers, not ranges Salary bands, fee structures, timelines — vagueness reads as hiding something, so we don't.
Engineers vet engineers Every technical screen and every code review is run by someone who does that job themselves.
Documented, not dependent Every engagement hands back systems your own team can run without us.
We say what we'd actually do Not a generic pitch — the specific thing we'd change about your pipeline, your suite, your req.
