08

Imagery rules

product / dashboard shot
Real UI, first choice
Screenshots of actual pipelines, coverage reports, dashboards. De-identified. Never a fabricated UI overlay.
abstract diagram
Illustrated, for services
Pipeline and architecture diagrams in navy on off-white, amber for the one highlighted path. No 3D, no glow.
candid photo
People pages only
Natural light, real tools in hand, no staged handshakes or laptop smiles. About page ships text-only until real photos exist.
Hard rules
No fake client logos. No stock team photos with invented names. No stat without a source in the CMS. Every image needs alt text before publish. Aspect ratios: 16:9 hero, 3:2 card, 1:1 avatar.
09

Iconography

One icon set, line only. Icons label, they never decorate.

Specification
Set — Lucide, outline only. No mixing sets, no filled variants.
Sizes — 16 inline with text, 20 in buttons and list items, 24 in cards, 32 max.
Stroke — 1.5px at every size. Never scaled to fake weight.
Color — inherits text color. Amber only when the adjacent text is also the active state.
Alignment — optical center to the cap height of its label, 8px gap.
Where icons are allowed
Service and engagement-model list items
Job card metadata: location, type, posted date
Form validation, accordion chevrons, nav affordances
Not allowed: icons as section ornament, icon-only buttons without labels, illustrated icon badges in circles
