Flexible Engagement Models

Your challenge defines how we work together — not the other way round.

01 Project Based

Fixed scope, fixed outcomes, milestone-based delivery. Best when you know what you need built and when it needs to ship.

Best when: scope and deadline are both fixed Typical length: single delivery, milestone-billed
02 Time & Materials

Flexible capacity for evolving scope. Scale the team up or down as requirements change, billed against actual hours.

Best when: requirements will change as you learn Typical length: rolling, month-to-month
03 Dedicated Team

A fully committed team aligned to your objectives, deeply integrated with your culture, standups and tooling.

Best when: you need an outcome owned end-to-end Typical length: 6+ months, renewable
04 Staff Augmentation

Specialist skills embedded directly in your existing team, faster than hiring — and without the overhead of a full-time role.

Best when: you know exactly what skill is missing Typical length: 2 weeks to onboard, flexible after
