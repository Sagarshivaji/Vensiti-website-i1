Questions people ask before signing

What happens to our documentation when you leave?+

It stays with you. Every runbook, diagram and decision record lives in your own repo or wiki from day one — nothing is held back at handover.

Do your engineers work in our repo or theirs?+

Yours. We work inside your existing repositories and tooling rather than standing up a parallel system you'd have to migrate away from later.

How do you handle access, secrets and compliance?+

Least-privilege access scoped to the engagement, revoked on completion, with secrets managed through your existing vault — never copied elsewhere.

Can we stop after the assessment?+

Yes. The findings are yours either way — there's no obligation to continue into implementation.
