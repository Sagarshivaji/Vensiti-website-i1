Questions

Before You Ask.

Do we have to choose between DevOps and Quality Engineering?+

No. Most engagements start with whichever is causing the most pain right now — the assessment often surfaces where the other practice would help too.

What if we're not sure which problem is the real one?+

That's exactly what the "Talk it through" call and the two-week assessment are for — naming the real problem is often the hardest part, and it's the first thing we do together.

Do you work with our existing tools, or want us to switch?+

We work inside what you already run. We only recommend changing tooling when the tooling itself — not how it's used — is the actual constraint.

What happens after the two-week assessment?+

You get findings ranked by cost of not fixing, yours to keep either way. Continuing into staff augmentation, a managed pod, or project delivery is optional, not assumed.
